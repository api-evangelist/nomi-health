# Nomi Health

> Nomi Health is a Utah-based direct healthcare and healthcare fintech company founded in 2019 that operates a payments, claims and analytics platform for self-funded employers, health plans, brokers and providers. Its stack spans real-time provider payments and claims adjudication (Healthcare Financial Services), directly contracted provider networks (Direct Network / Open Network), benefits-data analytics through Artemis by Nomi Health, and direct patient care services. Nomi Health operates authenticated web portals for providers, members and employers, but publishes no public developer program, API reference, or machine-readable specification. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the public Nomi Health website; it is a third-party profile, not a Nomi Health publication.

## Solutions
- [Healthcare Financial Services](https://www.nomihealth.com/business/healthcare-financial-services): Payments platform built for healthcare — real-time provider payments, claims adjudication and billing workflows.
- [Analytics — Artemis by Nomi Health](https://www.nomihealth.com/business/analytics): Benefits-data analytics for buyers of care; plan, manage and measure a benefits strategy.
- [Direct Network](https://www.nomihealth.com/business/direct-network): Direct provider contracts powered by Nomi Health infrastructure.
- [Open Network — for physicians](https://www.nomihealth.com/business/open-network/for-physicians): Provider-side participation in the Nomi Health open network.
- [Open Network — for members](https://www.nomihealth.com/business/open-network/for-members): Member-side access to the open network.
- [Digital Health Solutions](https://www.nomihealth.com/business/digital-health-solutions): Digital health offerings for employers and plans.
- [Care Delivery](https://www.nomihealth.com/patients/care-delivery): Direct patient care services.

## Notes for agents
- Do not confuse Nomi Health (nomihealth.com, healthcare payments) with Nomi.ai (nomi.ai, AI companion product), which does publish a REST API at api.nomi.ai. They are unrelated companies.
- Nomi Health handles PHI as a HIPAA covered entity; any integration is transacted through contracts and authenticated portals rather than a self-service developer program.
